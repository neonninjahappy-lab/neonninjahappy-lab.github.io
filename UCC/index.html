What is Ultimate Coin Collectors?

UCC is an upcoming Roblox simulator game inspired by the Pet Simulator series, bringing you endless fun in collecting, hatching, and exploring!

Dive into a vibrant world where every coin counts! Ultimate Coin Collectors offers a unique blend of classic simulator mechanics with fresh, original features. Smash giant coins to gather smaller ones, discover rare rewards, and hatch adorable pets that boost your collecting power.

Explore diverse worlds, each with their own areas, pets, and treasures. UCC is still in development, with new systems, maps, and content being created as development continues.

🛠️ UCC Development Journey

A look back at some of the biggest milestones in the development of Ultimate Coin Collectors.

The Beginning

December 2025

UCC began with early brainstorming, UI work, and the creation of the first world. The foundation of the game started to take shape.

Building the First World

January 2026

The first world was developed alongside the early pet system. Coin spawning and coin breaking were also added, creating the foundation of the main gameplay loop.

Expanding UCC

February 2026

Development continued with UI improvements, bug fixes, World 2, and new pets for the worlds. This was an important step toward expanding the game beyond its original foundation.

A Fresh Start

July 2026

After a long development break, UCC was restarted from scratch. The old version was removed and development began again with a new, higher-performance coin system.

The New Era of UCC

August 2026 - Present

Development accelerated with new UI, a new pet system, the UCC website, and a new developer joining the project. Map 1 and many parts of the game's UI were also revamped.
