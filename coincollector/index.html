What is UCC?

Ultimate Coin Collectors is a Roblox simulator built around satisfying coin-breaking, collecting, pets and world progression.

In UCC, players break large coin objects around each world. When a coin is broken, it splits into smaller collectibles that can be picked up to grow your progress.

Pets help you collect faster by giving you multipliers, while new worlds provide bigger challenges and better rewards. The game is being built with more systems planned for future updates.

The goal: make collecting coins feel simple, satisfying and rewarding while giving players reasons to keep exploring.
