📖 About Ultimate Coin Collectors

A closer look at what UCC is, how it plays and what is being built.

🪙 The Core Loop

UCC centers around breaking large coin objects. Those objects produce smaller coins that players collect to make progress and unlock more of the game.

  • ✓ Break large coin objects
  • ✓ Collect the coins they drop
  • ✓ Use progression to unlock more areas

🐾 Pets & Multipliers

Pets are a major part of progression. Players can hatch pets and use their multipliers to make collecting more efficient.

  • ✓ Hatch pets from eggs
  • ✓ Equip pets to boost collecting
  • ✓ Build a stronger collection over time

🌎 Worlds & Progression

UCC is designed around unlocking new worlds and areas. More areas are being modeled and developed as the game grows.

  • ✓ Unlock new areas
  • ✓ Discover stronger rewards
  • ✓ Expand the world over time

⚡ Systems Being Built

The project goes beyond the basic simulator loop. More player and community systems are being added as development continues.

  • ✓ Trading
  • ✓ Codes and daily rewards
  • ✓ Gifting, events and prestige
Development status: UCC is still being actively developed. Features, layouts and progression may change as the game is tested and improved.
