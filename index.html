iOS Installation Steps:

1. Click Direct Install, then open Settings > General > VPN & Device Management and trust the profile.

2. After tapping Trust, a popup will ask to Allow and Restart. Tap "Allow and restart" to finish enabling the profile.
Why do I need to trust a profile?

CloudFurm isn't distributed through the App Store, so iOS needs an explicit, one-time permission (a "configuration profile") before it will run apps installed this way. Only trust profiles from sources you recognize.

FAQ
Is CloudFurm free to use?

Yes, downloading and using the editor is completely free.

Will this appear in the App Store or Play Store?

Not currently. CloudFurm is distributed directly by the developer via APK and iOS trusted profiles.

Can I use it offline?

Yes — your projects are saved on-device so you can edit anytime offline.
