iOS Installation Steps:

1. Click Direct Install, then open Settings > General > VPN & Device Management and trust the profile.

2. After tapping Trust, a popup will ask to Allow and Restart. Tap "Allow and restart" to finish enabling the profile.
Why do I need to trust a profile?

CloudFurm isn't distributed through the App Store, so iOS needs an explicit, one-time permission (a "configuration profile") before it will run apps installed this way. This is how CloudFurm reaches your device outside App Store review — only trust profiles from sources you actually recognize, since the same mechanism can be used to install unwanted software.

FAQ
Is CloudFurm free to use?

Yes, downloading and using the editor is free. Live collaboration runs on a shared relay, so very heavy usage may be rate-limited.

Will this appear in the App Store or Play Store?

Not currently. CloudFurm is distributed directly by the developer, which is why Android uses an APK and iOS uses a trusted profile instead of a store listing.

Can I use it without an internet connection?

Yes — your projects are saved on-device, so you can keep editing offline. Live collaboration and voice chat need a connection.

How do I uninstall it?

Android: remove it like any other app from your app drawer. iOS: delete the app, then optionally remove the trusted profile in Settings > General > VPN & Device Management.
